Claudia Rodriguez Meza

Technical VFX Artist

Hello! My name is Claudia and, as you have seen, I am a technical VFX artist for video games. In my spare time, I love doing exercise, reading, baking, and, obviously, playing video games.

My education

I have a Bachelor's in Digital Art with a minor in video games. During the four years of my degree, I was able to learn about traditional animation, 3D modeling, rigging, drawing, and programming. I am really proud to say I graduated with the highest GPA of my class with a grade of A+ or 97 out of 100.

After completing my bachelor's, I decided I wanted to specialize in video game programming and decided to look for opportunities outside of Mexico. That is how I got into Canada and into Red River Polytechnic College. I am currently in the second and last year of an advanced diploma in video game programming, which has been a great opportunity not only to gain technical abilities, but to meet new people and step outside my comfort zone.

My education: Programming

The advanced diploma is not my first approach to programming. I am proud to say that in 2024 I was granted a scholarship by Oracle to get certified in back-end programming for webpages and MySQL servers. This allowed me to gain experience in JavaScript, MySQL, and a basic understanding of HTML and CSS.

My education: Tech Support

Going through the diploma and getting to learn more about programming and computers made me realize that I not only love developing video games, but I am genuinely intrigued by how computers work and how all their components interact. I even decided to go a step ahead and completed the IT Specialist certification by Google. Doing this certification has allowed me to have a deeper understanding of tech support, computers, components, and the logic behind them. At the moment, I am even looking to get the CompTIA A+ certification.

My hobbies: Videogames

I not only love developing video games, but also playing them. I mostly play FPS, but I am always open to trying new genres. My favourite game of all time is BioShock. It was the first game that made me realize that video games are a medium for people to connect with the story, the characters, the world, and you are even able to make them part of the story itself. This is what happened to me with BioShock; I was so intrigued by the story that I wanted to learn more about the universe, the philosophy behind it, the studio, the game design and well... basically everything.

My second favourite game is Destiny 2. Even though it was revealed recently that the game has ended its live service, I have been playing it for over ten years and it has been one of the greatest experiences of my life. I love how complex the story and lore surrounding the universe is. I met incredible people thanks to the game, from which some became friends that have been in my life for more than seven years. On the other hand, another thing that really caught me was the VFX. I love so much the ultimate effects, enemies, guns, and environmental VFX. I have studied them multiple times, and I am always mesmerized by how complex and breathtaking they are.

My hobbies: Exercise

Another hobby I have is exercise. I love swimming, boxing, skating, running, walking, going to the gym, dancing, and basically anything related to moving and exercising. I love challenging myself and giving me the opportunity to try new things even if that means being a complete beginner or even failing. The best way to learn is by trying and sometimes failing along the way.
