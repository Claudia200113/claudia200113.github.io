Claudia Rodriguez Meza

Technical VFX Artist

Holi! My name is Claudia and as you have seen I am a technical artist for videogames. I am a proud Mexican who love doing excercise, paiting, reading, baking and obviosly playing videogames.

My education

I have a bachelors in Digital Art with a minor in videogames. During the four years of the bachelors I was able to learn about traditional animation, 3D modeling, rigging, drawing, and programming. I am really proud to say I graduated as the highest GPA of my program.

After completing my bachelors, I decided I wanted to specialize in videogame programming and decided to look for oportunities outside of Mexico and that is how I got here into Canada and into Red River College. The advace diploma has been a great oppoerunity not only to gain technical abilities, but to get to know new people, challenge my self in creating complex projects, and always steping out of my comfort zone to learn more.

My hobbies: Videogames

I not only love doing videogames, but also playing them. I mostly play FPS, but I am always open to try new games and genres. My favourite game of all times is Bioshock. It was the first game that made me realize that videogames are a medium for people to connect, create a world, stories, and even make the player connect personally with the story and characters. This is what happend to me with Bioshock, I was so intrigued by the story that I wanted to learn more about the universe, the philosophy behing it, the studio, designes and well... everything basically.

My second favourtite game is Destiny 2. Eventhough it was revelead recently that the game has ended its live service, I have been playing this title for over ten years and it has been one of the greates exprience of my life. I love how complete and complex the story and lore sorrounding the universe is. Another thing that really catched me were the VFX. I love so much the ultimate effects, enemies, guns, and environmental VFX. I have studied them multiple times and I am always mesmerized by how complex and breath taking they are.

My hobbies: Excercise

Another hooby I have is excercise. I love swimming, boxing, skating, running, walking, going to the gym, dancing, and basically anything that is related to moving and excercising.
