Claudia Rodriguez Meza

Technical VFX Artist

Hello! My name is Claudia and as you have seen, I am a technical VFX artist for videogames. But more than that, I am a proud Mexican who loves doing excercise, reading, baking and obviously playing videogames.

My education

I have a bachelors in Digital Art with a minor in videogames. During the four years of the bachelors I was able to learn about traditional animation, 3D modeling, rigging, drawing, and programming. I am really proud to say I graduated as the highest GPA of my program.

After completing my bachelors, I decided I wanted to specialize in videogame programming and decided to look for opportunities outside of Mexico. That is how I got into Canada and into Red River Polytechnic College. I am studying and advanced diploma in videogame programming, which has been a great opportunity not only to gain technical abilities, but to get to know new people, challenge my self in creating complex projects, and stepping outside of my comfort zone.

Studying the diploma and getting to learn more about programming made me realize that I not only love developing videogames, but I am genuinly intrigued by how computers work and how all their components interact. As I wanted to learn more about this, I decided to go a step ahead and I am going through the IT specialist certification by Google.

My hobbies: Videogames

I not only love developing videogames, but also playing them. I mostly play FPS, but I am always open to trying new genres. My favourite game of all times is Bioshock. It was the first game that made me realize that videogames are a medium for people to connect with the story, the characters, the world, and you are even able to make them part of the story itself. This is what happend to me with Bioshock, I was so intrigued by the story that I wanted to learn more about the universe, the philosophy behing it, the studio, the game design and well... basically everything.

My second favourtite game is Destiny 2. Even though it was revelead recently that the game has ended its live service, I have been playing it for over ten years and it has been one of the greates exprience of my life. I love how complete and complex the story and lore sorrounding the universe is. As well, I met incredible persons thanks to the game, friends that have been in my life for more than seven years. On the other hand, the another thing that really catched me were the VFX. I love so much the ultimate effects, enemies, guns, and environmental VFX. I have studied them multiple times and I am always mesmerized by how complex and breath taking they are.

My hobbies: Excercise

Another hooby I have is excercise. I love swimming, boxing, skating, running, walking, going to the gym, dancing, and basically anything that is related to moving and excercising. I love challenging myself and giving me the opportunity to try new things even if that means being a complete beginner or even failing. The best way to learn is by trying and sometimes failing along the way.
