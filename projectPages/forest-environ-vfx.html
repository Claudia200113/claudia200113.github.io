Stylized Forest

Stylized Forest Scene

Overview

For this project, I decided to create a fully stylized forest environment. I worked on the lighting, environmental effects, skybox, and fog to create a cozy atmosphere to complement the overall art style.

I set up all the scene lighting, including the streetlights, bonfire, and skybox. I also created and implemented stylized smoke effects to enhance the atmosphere. Finally, I worked on setting up the renders and sequencers to capture the final media.
