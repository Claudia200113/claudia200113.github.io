Dark Matter Boost

A fan made version of Rocket League's Dark Matter Boost.

Overview

For this project, I wanted to learn more about working with ribbons and one off the best example I could think of was Rocket League's boosts. I decided to recreate my own version of the dark matter boost because, at first I had no idea how to work around the lightning ribbon and the dark central trail.

Recreating the effect was an exciting challenge as I got to learn and put on practice new concepts, while still breaking down and analyzing each element to figure out how all pieces work together. I wanted to not only to achieve the original boost look, but also gain a deep understanding of ribbons inside of Niagara, while working towards achieving Rocket League's high visual quality.
