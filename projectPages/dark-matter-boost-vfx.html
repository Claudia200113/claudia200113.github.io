Contrail VFX

A fan made version of Rocket League's Dark Matter Boost

Overview

For this project, I wanted to leanr more about working with ribbons and one off the best example I could think of was Rocket League's boosts. I decided to recreate my own version of the dark matter boost because, at first glance, I had no idea how to wotk around the lighting ribbons and the dark central trail.

I wanted to choose an effect that would push me out of my comfort zone and introduce me to new techniqies. Recreating the effect was an exciting challenge as I get to learn new concepts, and still requiere to break down wach element figuring out how all the pieces work together. My goal was not only to achieve the orginal boost energy look, but also gain a deep undestandign of ribbons inside of Niagara while wotking towards the high visual quality that Rocket League is know for.
