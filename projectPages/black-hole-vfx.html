Black Hole VFX

A visual reinterpretation of Zarya's ultimate ability from Overwatch.

Overview

I created this project as part of my Bachelor's Degree graduation project in Digital Art. The goal was to analyze and reinterpret some of the visual effects used in Overwatch, exploring how to enhance them and their overall impact on the gameplay. For this example, I chose Zarya's Graviton Surge.

Firstly I identified the anticipation phase of the effect could be improved. Compared to the duration and gameplay impact of the ability, the buildup felt a little subtle. Since this is Zarya's most powerful ability, players should be able to immediately recognize both the cast and the transition into the damage phase.
I decided to lengthen the charging time of the ultimate and introduce additional visual elements such as floating rubble, particle sprites, and distortion effects. These additions help communicate the growing force of the ultimate while providing clearer visual feedback to players.

For the climax I focused on increasing its sense of scale and power. I added debris which is pulled from the environment and drawn towards the center, giving a stronger impression of a black hole.

I also redesigned the dissipation phase to provide a clearer outro. Now particles and rubble burst to clearly sign the end of the damage phase, while creating a smooth transition into the fade-out and ending of the ultimate.

Additional Notes

For the render of the effect, I selected an environment with an art style similar to Overwatch. This allowed me to better showcase how the effect would integrate into an in-game setting and demonstrate the visual design decisions behind the textures, materials, meshes, and particle systems used throughout the project.
