Master Material For Dot Creation

Master modular material for dot creation.

Overview

As I have been gaining more experience in creating effects, I noticed that dot-based particles are a common element across different styles and games. So this time, I decided to develop a modular master material that could support multiple visual styles for dot creation.

The material was designed to be highly flexible, allowing artist to quickly adjust, personalize and test different looks while maintaining a single material. A variety of dot shapes, including simple dots, outlined dots, donut-shpaed dots, and standalone outlines are supported. Additionally, features such as masks, distortion, and dissolve effects can be enabled and customized to ahcieve multiple behaviours and visuals.

The main goal of this project was to provide artists with creative control while minimizing complex set ups. All properties can be adjusted through exposed parameters and the material can even be adapted so parameters are controlled dynamically inside of Niagara.

Learnings

One of the biggest challenges of this project was sucessfully combining multiple masks, color chanels, and opacity calculations into a single material. Working around these aspects helped me better undestand blending modes, channel packing, and how transparency behaves inside Unreal.

Another important aspect of the project was maintaining a clean and scalable material graph. I wanted the tool to adapt to as many effect as possible and remain open to future behaviour addition. I focused on designing it to be easily undestandable by artists and programmers alike, by clearly dividing the graph into sections, exposing only relevant parameters, and documenting the used nodes.

This master material allowed me to practice not only shader development, but also creation of production-friendly tools that prioritize usability, maintainability, and optimization techniques. I was able to create a master material that can be used across a range of effects, allowing artist to reduce setup and iteration time, as well as improving the overall optimization of the project.
