Water Shader

Water Shader in Unreal

Overview

This time I decided to create a stylized water shader that could easily adapt to different projects. My goal was to build a flexible material with multiple customizable features that artists can change depending on their needs.

The shader includes animated caustics, caustic distortion, customizable normal maps, and ripples that are driven by the Distance to Nearest Surface node, allowing the water to react to nearby geometry. I also exposed parameters for colors, opacity, textures, distortion intensity, wave speed, and other material properties so the look can be quickly adjusted through an instance.

This project was a great opportunity to continue learning more about customizable materials and shaders. My focus was not only on achieving a visually impresive result, but also on building a modular, reusable, and easy to customize tool.
