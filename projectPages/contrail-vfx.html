Contrail VFX

A fan made recreation of Fortnite's: Portal Particles

Overview

This time I decided to do a fan-made recreation of Fortnite's Portal Particles effect. My goal was to analyze an existing AAA FX and reproduce it as accurately as possible.

I chose this effect because it presented an interesting challenge from both a technical and artistic point of view. Beyond recreating the visuals, I approached the effect as if it was being used on the actual game. This meant focusing not only on visual fidelity, but also on readability, organization, and optimization within the Niagara system.

One of the most challenging aspects of the project was reproducing the behavior of the particle dots. Although they appear simple at first sight, each group follows unique movement patterns and timing variations. Replicating these subtle differences while maintaining the overall look and feel of the original effect was a challenge.

Techincal Features

A major focus throughout development of this effect was the optimization. The first version used six separate emitters to reproduce the different particles and behaviors.

After using spawning groups, arrays, and custom binding I was able to reduce the number to only three. Two for each ribbon (blue and pink) and another one which handles all the dots. This significantly reduced system complexity while improving maintainability and runtime performance.

The result (which if I may say, I am very proud of!) is an effect that matches the visual quality and motion of the original Fortnite effect, while adhering to complex optimization practices.
