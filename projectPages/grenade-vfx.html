Grenade VFX

A visual reinterpretation of Tracer's ultimate ability from Overwatch.

Overview

I created this project as part of my Bachelor's Degree graduation project in Digital Art. The goal was to analyze and reinterpret some of the visual effects used in Overwatch, exploring how to enhance them and their overall impact on the gameplay. For this example, I chose Tracer's Pulse Bomb.

After analyzing the original effect, I noticed that the anticipation phase can sometimes be easy to miss, causing enemy players to overllok the ultimate being cast gameplay feel unfair. To address this, I added assets to give the effect more visual presence. I included lighting sprites, rubble meshes and smoke. This helps make the ultimate feek more powerful and impactful.

For the climax of the effect, I changed the explosion color to red and oragne tones, as these colors are commonly associated with danger and damage in color theory. This helps players immediatly notice the ultimate and react to it.

Finally, for the fadeout I added a burst of particles that lingers longer that the rest of the effect. This gives the impression that the ultimate was strong enough to "destroy" the environment while creating a smooth transition for the effect to end.

Additional Notes

For the render of the effect, I selected an environment with an art style similar to Overwatch. This allowed me to better showcase how the effect would integrate into an in-game setting and demonstrate the visual design decisions behind the textures, materials, meshes, and particle systems used throughout the project.
