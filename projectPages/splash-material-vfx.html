Splash Material

Water material which offsets vertices based on their distance to the floor.

Overview

(Work In Progress)
This time I wanted to focus on materials. I decided to create a splash material that offsets vertices based on their distance to the floor. I designed it to resemble a water sphere, with wave-like movement along the edges.

The goal was to create a stylized water material that can be used in a map without the need for animations, using only World Position Offset and vertex manipulation. I wanted to focus on covering multiple behaviours in a single material, so instead of creating multiple materials everthing can be controlled through material instances.

Parameters such as color, fresnel intensity, splash distance, surface distance, offset, and wave intensity can be easily adjusted.
